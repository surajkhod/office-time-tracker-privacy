Office Time Tracker – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Office Time Tracker is a browser extension that helps users track in-office and work-from-home hours using their device location. We are committed to protecting user privacy.

2. Information Accessed

The extension accesses the following information:

  • Location: GPS coordinates and location accuracy, used to identify whether the user is at their configured office location or working remotely.
  • Device activity state: Active, idle and locked states, used to trigger location checks and distinguish locked periods from work-from-home periods.
  • Time-tracking records: Location status and timestamps used to calculate office hours, work-from-home hours and weekly summaries.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the extension's core functionality:

  • Determine in-office and work-from-home status.
  • Record time-tracking events.
  • Calculate attendance and weekly compliance summaries.
  • Handle device sleep, idle and locked states.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Tracking information is stored locally in the browser using browser storage APIs. The extension does not transmit location information or tracking records to external servers or third parties.

We do not sell user information or share it with third parties.

5. Data Security

Tracking information remains in the user's browser storage. Users should protect their browser profile and device with appropriate security measures.

6. Data Deletion and User Support

For assistance with clearing locally stored extension data, users can contact the developer using the email address below.
